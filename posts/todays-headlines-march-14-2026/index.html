    Today's Headlines - March 14, 2026

    March 14, 2026

    World News#

    US-Israel War With Iran Enters Third Week#

    The US-Israel military campaign against Iran, now in its 15th day, escalated sharply after US forces struck military targets on Kharg Island, the critical hub handling roughly 90 percent of Iran’s crude exports. President Trump said the US deliberately spared oil infrastructure but warned it could be targeted if Iran continues blocking the Strait of Hormuz. Iran’s military warned it could strike ports in the UAE in retaliation, and smoke was seen rising from a major energy installation in Fujairah.

    Lebanon Toll Climbs as Israeli Strikes Intensify#

    Israeli attacks have killed at least 773 people in Lebanon since March 2, according to the Lebanese Ministry of Public Health. A strike on a healthcare center in southern Lebanon killed 12 medical staff, drawing condemnation from medical organizations. Israeli shells also struck a UN peacekeeping base in southern Lebanon.

    US Embassy in Baghdad Hit by Drones#

    Iraq confirmed that a helipad at the US Embassy in Baghdad was struck by a missile, and two drones hit the compound as the broader regional conflict continues to widen beyond Iran’s borders.

    Politics#

    TSA Workers Miss First Full Paycheck as DHS Shutdown Drags On#

    Transportation Security Administration officers missed their first full paycheck on Friday as the partial Department of Homeland Security shutdown approached the one-month mark. Funding lapsed in mid-February amid a standoff between Republicans and Democrats over immigration enforcement reform. TSA absence rates have more than doubled during the shutdown, with some airports seeing more than half of officers calling out. Spring break travelers at major airports like Houston Hobby and New Orleans reported wait times of up to three hours.

    Senate Deadlocked Over DHS Funding#

    Democrats and Republicans remain at an impasse over reopening DHS. Democrats are demanding restrictions on immigration enforcement after two US citizens were fatally shot by federal immigration agents in Minneapolis, while Republicans have pushed for full DHS funding without conditions. The two sides blocked each other’s proposals on the Senate floor this week.

    Business & Economy#

    Oil Prices Surge Past $90 Amid Strait of Hormuz Closure#

    Brent crude surged above $98 per barrel this week, up more than $20 for the month, as the effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz disrupted global oil supply. The IEA estimates global oil supply plunged by 8 million barrels per day in March. Analysts warn prices could reach $150 per barrel if shipping through the strait is not restored soon.

    Meta Plans Major Layoffs to Fund AI Infrastructure#

    Meta is reportedly planning layoffs affecting a large portion of its workforce as the company redirects capital toward building massive AI computing infrastructure. The move reflects a broader trend in the tech sector of restructuring operations to finance the next wave of AI development.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI Surpasses $25 Billion in Annualized Revenue#

    OpenAI has surpassed $25 billion in annualized revenue and is reportedly taking early steps toward a public listing, potentially as soon as late 2026. Rival Anthropic is approaching $19 billion in annualized revenue. The figures signal that the market for advanced AI models has rapidly become one of the fastest-growing sectors in the technology industry.

    Apple Announces AI-Powered Siri Overhaul#

    Apple confirmed that a completely reimagined, AI-powered version of Siri is set to debut alongside iOS 26.4. The new Siri will feature on-screen awareness and cross-app integration, powered by a partnership with Google to utilize its Gemini AI model running on Apple’s Private Cloud Compute infrastructure.

    NVIDIA Heads Into GTC 2026 With Record Expectations#

    NVIDIA enters its annual GTC conference expected to outline how it plans to defend its lead as the AI market shifts from massive model training toward inference, orchestration, and agent-heavy workloads. The company also announced a $2 billion investment in Amsterdam-based Nebius and a strategic partnership with Coherent for next-generation data center optics.

    Weather#

    Record-Breaking March Heat Wave Hits Western US#

    A powerful and unusually early heat dome is settling over the western United States, with temperatures expected to be 20 to 30 degrees above normal for mid-March. The National Weather Service warned of a historic and dangerous heat event that could last 10 to 14 days, with some areas in the Southwest potentially reaching triple digits. Downtown Los Angeles hit 92 degrees on Thursday. The heat will further damage the already critically low mountain snowpack across the West.

    Cold Snap Coming for Eastern US#

    While the West bakes, the jet stream is diving south out of Canada and bringing cold air into the Midwest and East. Cities that saw highs in the 70s and 80s this week could see temperatures plunge into the 30s and 40s next week, with chances for snow returning to the Northeast through mid-to-late March.

    Culture#

    Pi Day and International Mathematics Day#

    March 14 is celebrated worldwide as Pi Day and International Mathematics Day, commemorating the mathematical constant 3.14159 and promoting mathematical education and awareness.

    Oscar Weekend Approaches#

    Hollywood is gearing up for the Academy Awards ceremony this weekend in Los Angeles. Forecasters anticipate a brief cooling trend for Sunday’s ceremony before the stronger heat wave resumes next week.
