    Tech & AI#

    Meta’s AI Troubles Deepen#

    Meta’s latest AI model, code named Avocado, is apparently being delayed until May instead of launching this month, nine months and $14.3 billion after hiring Alexandr Wang and forming an AI super team. Meta’s leadership has apparently discussed licensing Gemini from Google on a temporary basis until Avocado is up to snuff, highlighting tensions in the competitive AI landscape.

    Apple Celebrates 50 Years#

    Apple announced it will mark its 50th anniversary, celebrating five decades of thinking different and the innovations that have helped shape the way people connect, create, learn, and experience the world.

    Trump Administration Pushes AI Infrastructure#

    The Trump administration’s Genesis Mission is pushing to use AI and emerging technologies across energy, drug discovery, national security, and science, with ambitions that include quantum advances, a larger trained technical workforce, and a new supercomputer blueprint by the end of 2026.

    Government & Politics#

    TSA Workers Struggle During Government Shutdown#

    U.S. Transportation Security Administration security officers missed their first full paycheck Friday as a partial funding shutdown of the government approached the one-month mark. Fees paid by airline passengers keep piling up, even as airport security officers work without pay.

    Youth Trend: “Chinamaxxing”#

    Despite rising tensions between the world’s two largest economies, a growing number of young Americans are becoming captivated by China, as seen in the online trend “Chinamaxxing”.
