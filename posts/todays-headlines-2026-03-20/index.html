    Today's Headlines - March 20, 2026

    March 20, 2026

    World News#

    Congo and Rwanda agree to de-escalation steps after U.S.-hosted talks#

    Reuters reports that officials from the Democratic Republic of Congo and Rwanda agreed to coordinated measures to ease fighting in eastern Congo, including force disengagement and civilian protections. The talks were the first since recent U.S. sanctions on Rwandan military figures and are aimed at preventing a wider regional conflict.

    U.S. Politics#

    Senate showdown over the SAVE America Act heads toward likely deadlock#

    Reuters reports Senate Republicans are pressing ahead with debate on President Trump’s voter ID and proof-of-citizenship bill even though it lacks the 60 votes needed to pass. Democrats say the proposal would disenfranchise eligible voters, setting up the issue as a major midterm campaign fight.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI expands federal AI sales through Amazon Web Services#

    Reuters reports OpenAI signed a deal to provide AI models to U.S. government agencies via AWS for both classified and unclassified work. The agreement deepens OpenAI’s Pentagon footprint and highlights how cloud providers are becoming central in the race for government AI contracts.

    Business#

    IMF warns prolonged energy shock could lift inflation and weaken growth#

    Reuters reports the IMF said extended disruptions to oil and gas flows from the Iran war could push global inflation higher and slow output. The fund said a sustained 10% rise in energy prices can add about 0.4 percentage points to inflation while trimming growth.

    Weather#

    Powerful U.S. storm system drives blizzard conditions and severe storm risks#

    NBC News and CNN report that a sprawling March storm brought heavy snow and near-whiteout conditions in parts of the Midwest while severe thunderstorms threatened much of the East. The system caused widespread outages and major air travel disruptions with thousands of cancellations and delays.

    Culture#

    Oscars 2026 split top honors between “One Battle After Another” and “Sinners”#

    NPR reports “One Battle After Another” won Best Picture and Best Director, while “Sinners” took major acting and screenplay awards, including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan. The ceremony also marked the first year for the Academy’s new casting category.
