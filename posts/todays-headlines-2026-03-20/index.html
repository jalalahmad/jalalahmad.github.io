    World News#

    Israel and Iran conflict widens around Gulf energy sites#

    NPR reports Israel launched new strikes around Tehran while Iran continued attacks targeting Gulf energy infrastructure, including facilities tied to regional oil and gas exports. The escalation deepened concerns about broader regional spillover and global energy disruption.

    U.S. Politics#

    Bipartisan talks intensify as Homeland Security shutdown drags on#

    NBC News reports Senate negotiators and White House border czar Tom Homan held new closed-door talks as the Department of Homeland Security shutdown stretched past a month. Senate leaders signaled they could cancel recess if lawmakers fail to reach a funding deal quickly.

    Tech & AI#

    Amazon is reportedly developing an AI-centered smartphone comeback#

    Reuters reports Amazon is working on a new smartphone project, code-named “Transformer,” more than a decade after the Fire Phone failed. The device is expected to emphasize AI features and deeper integration with Alexa and Amazon services.

    Business#

    Global bond markets sell off as investors price in war-driven inflation#

    Reuters reports U.S. and European government bond yields jumped as investors reassessed inflation and rate-cut expectations amid elevated energy prices. Markets are increasingly betting central banks may keep policy tighter for longer if oil shocks persist.

    Weather#

    U.S. Southwest heat wave approaches a potential all-time March record#

    CNN reports parts of California and Arizona climbed to around 108°F, tying or nearing the highest March temperatures ever recorded in the United States. Forecasters and climate researchers said human-driven warming is amplifying the severity and timing of this early-season heat.

    Culture#

    BTS returns with a new album and major Seoul comeback performance#

    NPR and NBC News report BTS released “Arirang,” its first group album in nearly four years, and planned a large comeback concert at Seoul’s Gwanghwamun Square. The release marks the group’s full return after military-service hiatuses and has drawn massive global fan attention.
