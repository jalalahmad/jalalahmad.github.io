    World News#

    Congo and Rwanda agree to de-escalation steps after Washington talks#

    Reuters reports that representatives from the Democratic Republic of Congo and Rwanda agreed to coordinated steps to reduce tensions, including force disengagement measures and civilian protections in eastern Congo. The meeting was the first since U.S. sanctions on Rwandan military officials and comes amid concern that fighting around M23-held territory could broaden into a regional conflict.

    US Politics#

    U.S. intelligence chiefs face Senate scrutiny over Iran war strategy#

    At a Senate Intelligence Committee hearing, officials said Iran’s government appears degraded but still intact, while lawmakers from both parties pressed for clearer answers on war goals and risk assessments. The hearing also highlighted the resignation of a senior counterterrorism official who publicly disagreed with the administration’s rationale for the conflict.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI moves to acquire Python toolmaker Astral#

    Reuters says OpenAI plans to buy Astral to strengthen its coding stack and compete more directly with Anthropic in AI developer tools. OpenAI said its Codex platform has surpassed 2 million weekly active users, signaling rapid growth in enterprise coding adoption.

    Business#

    Oil spike reshapes Fed rate expectations#

    With oil prices jumping above $118 per barrel amid Middle East escalation, markets have pulled back expectations for U.S. rate cuts and started pricing in a greater chance of a hike by year-end. Federal Reserve officials maintained a cautious stance, warning that the inflation and growth impact will depend on how long energy disruptions last.

    Weather#

    Major U.S. storm system brings blizzards, severe winds, and Hawaii flooding#

    NBC News reports a multi-region weather event has produced heavy snow and near-blizzard conditions in parts of the Upper Midwest while severe flooding and landslides have hit parts of Hawaii. The same system disrupted travel nationwide, with thousands of flight cancellations and widespread power outages.

    Record-setting March heat wave expands across the West#

    CNN reports an unusually strong heat dome is driving temperatures 20 to 30 degrees above normal across large parts of the West, with some cities setting all-time March highs. Forecasters warn the early-season heat could worsen snowpack loss, water stress, and wildfire risk heading into spring and summer.

    Culture#

    Oscars spread awards between ‘One Battle After Another’ and ‘Sinners’#

    NPR and Reuters report that the 2026 Oscars crowned “One Battle After Another” as best picture while “Sinners” earned major acting and craft wins, including best actor for Michael B. Jordan. The ceremony also featured several notable firsts, including the debut of the Academy’s new casting award category.
