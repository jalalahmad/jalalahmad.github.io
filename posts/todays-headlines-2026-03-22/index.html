    World News#

    Iran war enters a volatile fourth week as energy and security risks rise#

    Reuters reports the U.S.-Iran conflict has widened with oil shipments disrupted and more U.S. forces moving toward the region. Analysts say Washington faces rising pressure from high energy prices, allied friction, and the absence of a clear diplomatic off-ramp.

    U.S. Politics#

    Trump says ICE agents will be deployed to airports during DHS shutdown standoff#

    NBC News reports President Trump said ICE personnel will be sent to airports while Senate Republicans and Democrats remain deadlocked over DHS funding and ICE policy reforms. The standoff has left many TSA workers unpaid and contributed to long screening lines across major airports.

    Tech & AI#

    Nvidia raises AI chip opportunity forecast to $1 trillion through 2027#

    Reuters reports Nvidia used its GTC conference to unveil new inference-focused systems and raise its projected AI chip revenue opportunity from $500 billion to $1 trillion through 2027. The company is emphasizing real-time AI inference as competition intensifies from rivals and custom chips.

    Business#

    U.S. regulators propose softer bank-capital rewrite in major win for lenders#

    Reuters reports federal regulators unveiled new capital proposals that would reduce required capital for Wall Street banks by about 4.8% versus current frameworks. Supporters say the change could free up lending capacity, while critics warn it may weaken post-crisis safeguards.

    Weather#

    Catastrophic Oahu flooding triggers evacuations and hundreds of rescues#

    CNN reports severe flooding on Oahu prompted evacuation orders near the Wahiawa dam and forced emergency crews to rescue more than 200 people. Officials warned damage could exceed $1 billion statewide as more heavy rain threatens already saturated areas.

    Culture#

    BTS returns with first full-group concert in nearly four years#

    CNN reports BTS staged a free Seoul comeback show tied to its new album “Arirang,” with organizers expecting one of the largest public concert turnouts in South Korean history. The performance marks the group’s first major live appearance together since its hiatus and launches a global tour.
