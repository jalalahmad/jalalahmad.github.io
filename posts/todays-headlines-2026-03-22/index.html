    March 22, 2026

    World News#

    U.S.-Iran conflict escalates with threats to energy and water infrastructure#

    Reuters reports President Trump threatened strikes on Iranian power plants if the Strait of Hormuz is not fully reopened, while Iran warned it could target U.S.-linked infrastructure and Gulf desalination facilities. The exchange signals a broader regional escalation risk and deepens concerns over energy and water security.

    U.S. Politics#

    Trump says ICE could be sent to airports as DHS funding fight drags on#

    NBC News reports the White House is threatening to deploy ICE agents to airports while Senate negotiations over Department of Homeland Security funding remain stalled. TSA workers remain unpaid during the shutdown period, and long screening lines are increasing pressure on lawmakers to reach a deal.

    Tech & AI#

    White House unveils federal AI legislation framework#

    NBC News reports the administration released a seven-part AI policy framework that calls for a single national approach, child-safety safeguards, and limits on developer liability. The proposal sets up a major federal-state fight over how aggressively AI companies should be regulated.

    Business#

    U.S. temporarily eases Iranian oil sanctions to cool price shock#

    NBC News reports the Treasury moved to temporarily allow sales of Iranian oil stranded at sea to add supply and ease pressure from surging fuel prices. Analysts say the relief could be limited if shipping disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz continue.

    Weather#

    Severe Hawaii flooding triggers evacuations and hundreds of rescues#

    NBC News and AP report that a powerful Kona low brought Hawaii’s worst flooding in about two decades, prompting evacuations on Oahu and warnings over an aging dam. Officials say more than 200 people were rescued and additional flash-flood risk remains across multiple islands.

    Culture#

    BTS comeback concert sets attendance record in South Korea#

    CNN and NBC News report BTS returned for its first major concert in years with a Seoul show described as the largest public concert ever held in South Korea. The event is being treated as a major pop-culture moment with global fan attention.
