    March 22, 2026

    World News#

    Trump and Iran exchange new threats over Gulf energy targets#

    Reuters reports President Donald Trump warned he could strike Iranian power plants if Tehran does not fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours. Iran responded by threatening U.S.-linked infrastructure in the region, raising fears of wider disruption to global energy supplies.

    U.S. Politics#

    Republicans push back on Democrats’ demand for public Iran war hearings#

    NBC News reports Democratic lawmakers are calling for open hearings on U.S. war goals, including whether ground troops or broader operations are planned. Republican leadership has largely resisted, arguing public hearings during the operation could compromise military strategy.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI says ads are coming to all U.S. free and Go ChatGPT users#

    Reuters reports OpenAI plans to roll out ads to all U.S. users on its free and Go tiers in the coming weeks. The company is expanding advertising as it seeks new revenue streams to offset rising infrastructure costs in the AI race.

    Business#

    Fed holds rates steady and signals inflation risk remains elevated#

    Reuters reports the Federal Reserve kept its benchmark rate unchanged at 3.50% to 3.75% while projecting higher inflation this year. Chair Jerome Powell said policymakers face unusually high uncertainty from energy-market shocks tied to the conflict in Iran.

    Weather#

    Massive U.S. storm system brings tornado risk, blizzard conditions, and major travel disruptions#

    CNN reports a sprawling storm system has produced severe thunderstorms and tornado warnings across parts of the East while dropping feet of snow in the Upper Midwest. The system has also caused widespread power outages and thousands of flight delays and cancellations.

    Culture#

    BTS returns with reunion album “Arirang” after a four-year group hiatus#

    NPR’s review says BTS’ new album marks a major comeback after the group’s military-service break and reasserts its influence on global pop. The release is framed as both a cultural reset for the band and a new chapter in K-pop’s international reach.
