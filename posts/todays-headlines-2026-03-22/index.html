    Today's Headlines - March 22, 2026

    March 22, 2026

    World News#

    Trump threatens strikes on Iranian power plants over Hormuz blockade#

    Reuters reports President Trump warned that the U.S. would attack and “obliterate” major Iranian power plants if Tehran does not fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours. Iranian military officials responded by threatening U.S.-linked energy, desalination, and technology infrastructure across the region.

    US Politics#

    Senate battle builds over Trump-backed SAVE America Act#

    Reuters reports the proposed law would require in-person proof of citizenship for voter registration, stricter photo ID rules, and expanded federal checks of state voter rolls. The measure is expected to face a tough path in the Senate, with critics warning it could disenfranchise eligible voters who lack key documents.

    Republicans resist calls for public hearings on Iran war strategy#

    NBC News reports Democratic lawmakers are pressing for open hearings on U.S. war aims in Iran, including questions about possible ground troop deployments. Republican leaders have mostly rejected those demands so far, arguing that public hearings could undermine military operations.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI reportedly plans to nearly double workforce by end of 2026#

    Reuters, citing the Financial Times, reports OpenAI plans to expand headcount from roughly 4,500 to about 8,000 employees by late 2026. The hiring push is expected to focus on engineering, research, product development, sales, and technical advisory roles for enterprise customers.

    Business & Markets#

    Energy shock pushes S&P 500 into a 5% pullback#

    Reuters reports the S&P 500 has fallen more than 5% from its January high as higher oil prices and inflation fears increase market volatility. Analysts say similar drops often recover, but investors remain cautious that the downturn could deepen if the conflict and supply disruptions persist.

    Weather#

    Catastrophic flooding on Oahu triggers mass rescues and evacuations#

    CNN reports more than 230 people were rescued as severe flooding cut off parts of Oahu and prompted evacuation orders near the Wahiawa dam. Hawaii officials said damage could top $1 billion as additional rain threatens already saturated communities.

    Culture#

    BTS returns with largest public concert ever held in South Korea#

    CNN reports BTS performed together for the first time in nearly four years at a major comeback concert in Seoul tied to the release of its new album, Arirang. Organizers expected crowds far beyond the ticketed audience, marking one of the biggest live pop culture events of the year.
