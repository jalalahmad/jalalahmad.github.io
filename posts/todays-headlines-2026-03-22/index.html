    World News#

    Ceasefire diplomacy stalls as Iran war widens and oil pressure builds#

    Reuters reports the Trump administration rejected new mediation efforts to open Iran ceasefire talks, while Tehran also refused negotiations unless U.S. and Israeli strikes stop first. The diplomatic deadlock is deepening concern over civilian casualties and prolonged disruption in the Strait of Hormuz.

    U.S. Politics#

    Senate fight over Trump’s voting bill intensifies despite slim path to passage#

    Reuters reports the SAVE America Act remains a top White House priority but is expected to fall short in the Senate under the 60-vote threshold. The bill would require in-person proof of citizenship for voter registration and impose new federal and state enforcement requirements.

    Tech & AI#

    U.S. considers new AI chip export framework with tougher conditions#

    Reuters reports U.S. officials are weighing rules that could tie large AI chip exports to foreign investment in American data centers or formal security assurances. The proposal would mark a major shift from earlier policies that gave broader exemptions to close U.S. allies.

    Business#

    Markets retreat as Fed holds rates and energy-driven inflation risks rise#

    Reuters reports global stocks fell after the Federal Reserve kept interest rates unchanged and projected limited easing this year. Oil prices climbed sharply and hotter U.S. producer inflation data added to investor concern about growth and pricing pressures.

    Weather#

    U.S. logs new all-time March heat record during exceptional western heat wave#

    CNN reports Yuma, Arizona reached 109 degrees, breaking the previous U.S. March record and capping a week of widespread record highs across the West. Meteorologists said the early-season heat dome was intensified by human-caused climate warming and could keep dangerous conditions in place for days.

    Culture#

    Oscars 2026 spread top honors across major contenders#

    Reuters reports One Battle After Another led the ceremony with six wins, including Best Picture and Best Director, while Sinners followed with four major awards. The night also featured milestone wins in acting, cinematography, and music categories.
