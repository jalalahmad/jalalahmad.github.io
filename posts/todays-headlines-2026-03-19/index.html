    Today's Headlines - March 19, 2026

    March 19, 2026

    World News#

    EU leaders press Hungary to unblock a major Ukraine loan package#

    Reuters reports that European Union leaders are pushing Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán to lift his veto on a 90-billion-euro loan for Ukraine. Officials say Kyiv may face a funding shortfall within weeks if the package remains stalled.

    Trump and Japan’s prime minister meet under pressure from the Iran conflict#

    Reuters reports President Donald Trump is expected to seek more allied support to secure shipping lanes near the Strait of Hormuz during talks with Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi. The meeting comes as global energy markets react to escalating Middle East strikes.

    U.S. Politics#

    Senate opens debate on the SAVE America Act, but passage remains unlikely#

    NBC News reports Senate Republicans advanced debate on President Trump’s election bill that would tighten voter ID and citizenship requirements. The bill still appears short of the 60 votes needed to clear the chamber.

    Tech & AI#

    Nvidia says AI chip revenue opportunity could reach $1 trillion#

    Reuters reports Nvidia said AI chip demand through 2027 could total at least $1 trillion as more companies shift from training models to running real-time AI inference. The company also unveiled new chip and system plans aimed at defending its lead.

    Meta signs a multibillion-dollar AI capacity deal with Nebius#

    Reuters reports Nebius said it signed agreements to provide Meta with $12 billion in AI computing capacity by 2027, with possible expansion up to $27 billion over five years. The deal highlights how major tech firms are racing to lock in scarce data center resources.

    Business#

    Federal Reserve holds rates steady as energy-driven inflation risks rise#

    Reuters reports the Fed left rates unchanged and projected only limited easing, while warning that higher energy prices could keep inflation elevated. Markets have increasingly pushed expectations for rate cuts further out.

    Weather#

    Extreme weather hits much of the U.S. with blizzards, storms, and heat#

    NPR reports more than half the country has faced severe weather impacts, including blizzard conditions in parts of the Midwest, heavy rain in Hawaii, and triple-digit heat in the Southwest. The system has disrupted travel and triggered thousands of flight cancellations and delays.

    Culture#

    Oscars 2026 spread major awards across several films#

    NPR reports “One Battle After Another” won Best Picture and Best Director, while “Sinners” claimed major acting and screenplay wins, including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan. The ceremony also introduced the Academy’s first Oscar category for casting.
