    World News#

    Trump criticizes NATO allies for refusing to join Iran operation#

    Reuters reports President Trump said most NATO allies declined to participate militarily in the Iran conflict, calling the decision a major strategic mistake. The split has intensified tensions over burden-sharing and maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz.

    Russia says it repelled one of the biggest drone attacks on Moscow in a year#

    Reuters reports Russian officials said air defenses intercepted about 250 drones approaching Moscow over two days. The escalation highlights how long-range drone warfare is widening risks for major population and transport hubs.

    U.S. Politics#

    Senate battle over Trump-backed voting restrictions heads toward likely gridlock#

    Reuters and NPR report Senate Republicans are pushing the SAVE America Act, which would require proof of citizenship and photo ID for federal voting, while Democrats call it voter suppression. With Republicans short of 60 votes, the measure appears unlikely to pass.

    Tech & AI#

    Nvidia says AI infrastructure opportunity could hit $1 trillion through 2027#

    Reuters reports Nvidia used its GTC conference to project a much larger revenue opportunity as demand shifts from model training to inference at scale. The company also unveiled new CPU and system plans aimed at defending share as competition increases.

    Microsoft reportedly weighs legal action over OpenAI’s cloud deal with Amazon#

    Reuters reports Microsoft is considering legal options over a reported $50 billion Amazon-OpenAI agreement it believes may conflict with existing cloud exclusivity terms. The dispute underscores rising pressure and high stakes in enterprise AI infrastructure contracts.

    Business#

    Oil settles sharply higher as renewed Gulf attacks raise supply fears#

    Reuters reports Brent crude settled above $103 after fresh attacks near UAE energy infrastructure and continued disruption around the Strait of Hormuz. Traders are pricing in a longer risk window for global energy supply and inflation.

    Weather#

    Powerful U.S. storm system brings blizzard conditions, severe thunderstorms, and flight disruptions#

    CNN reports a sprawling March storm swept across multiple regions with heavy snow in parts of the Midwest and Great Lakes, while severe storms threatened over 100 million people in the East. High winds and extreme conditions led to widespread travel delays and power outages.

    Culture#

    Oscars 2026: “One Battle After Another” wins Best Picture as “Sinners” lands major wins#

    NPR and CNN report the Academy Awards split top honors, with “One Battle After Another” taking Best Picture and Best Director while “Sinners” earned major acting and screenplay victories. The ceremony also included historic recognition in several craft and music categories.
