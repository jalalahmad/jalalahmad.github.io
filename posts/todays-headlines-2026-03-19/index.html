    World News#

    Rwanda and Congo agree to new de-escalation steps in U.S.-hosted talks#

    Reuters reports that officials from the Democratic Republic of Congo and Rwanda agreed to coordinated measures aimed at lowering tensions in eastern Congo, including steps tied to force disengagement and civilian protection. The talks were the first since recent U.S. sanctions on Rwandan defense officials and come amid warnings of a wider regional conflict.

    US Politics#

    FBI opens leak probe tied to former counterterrorism chief Joe Kent#

    NBC News reports that the FBI is investigating former National Counterterrorism Center director Joe Kent over alleged classified leaks, and that the probe began before his resignation over U.S. policy in Iran. The development adds pressure to an already sharp internal debate inside the administration over the war’s rationale and strategy.

    Tech & AI#

    Nvidia says AI chip opportunity could reach $1 trillion by 2027#

    Reuters reports Nvidia told investors its AI chip revenue opportunity could top $1 trillion through 2027 as the market shifts from training models to real-time inference. The company also unveiled new chip and system plans designed to defend its lead as competition from custom AI hardware grows.

    Business#

    EU leaders push Hungary to lift veto on major Ukraine loan package#

    Reuters reports European Union leaders are pressing Hungary’s Viktor Orbán to unblock a 90-billion-euro loan meant to help finance Ukraine’s war effort. Diplomats say the standoff has raised concerns about EU credibility as Kyiv faces a near-term funding crunch.

    Weather#

    Massive U.S. storm system drives blizzard alerts, tornado risk, and flight chaos#

    NBC News reports tens of millions of people were under winter and severe weather alerts as heavy snow hit the Upper Midwest and dangerous storms threatened parts of the East Coast. The storm system also triggered widespread power outages and thousands of flight cancellations and delays.

    Culture#

    Oscars 2026 spread wins across major films with several historic moments#

    NPR reports “One Battle After Another” won Best Picture and Best Director, while “Sinners” earned major wins including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan. The ceremony also featured a rare tie in live-action short and the first-ever Oscar awarded for casting.
