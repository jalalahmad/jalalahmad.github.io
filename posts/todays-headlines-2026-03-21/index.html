    World News#

    Israel broadens strikes in Iran and Lebanon as U.S. sends more Marines#

    Reuters reports Israel launched new strikes in Tehran and Beirut while Washington deployed additional Marines and naval assets to the region. The widening conflict and disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz are intensifying fears of a broader regional war and prolonged energy-market turbulence.

    U.S. Politics#

    Bipartisan DHS funding talks resume as shutdown strains airports#

    NBC News and CNN report Senate negotiators and White House officials held new talks to end the Homeland Security shutdown, but both parties remain far apart on immigration-enforcement limits. With TSA staffing stretched, major airports continue to face longer lines and delays during heavy travel periods.

    Tech & AI#

    OpenAI expands U.S. government business through AWS#

    Reuters reports OpenAI signed a deal to provide AI models to U.S. defense and government agencies through Amazon Web Services for classified and unclassified work. The agreement deepens competition among major AI vendors for federal and national-security contracts.

    Business#

    Oil shock keeps markets on edge as inflation worries return#

    Fortune and Reuters report stocks have come under pressure as oil prices surged above $100 per barrel and investors reassessed the path for interest-rate cuts. Higher energy costs are raising concern about stickier inflation and slower global growth.

    Weather#

    Cross-country storm system drives severe weather, blizzard conditions, and travel disruption#

    CNN reports a powerful storm brought tornado and damaging-wind risks across the East while parts of the Upper Midwest and Great Lakes saw blizzard conditions and heavy snowfall. The system also triggered widespread flight delays, power outages, and hazardous road conditions.

    Culture#

    NPR reports “One Battle After Another” won Best Picture and Best Director, while “Sinners” earned major awards including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan and a historic cinematography win for Autumn Durald Arkapaw. The ceremony also featured a rare tie in live-action short film and marked the debut year for the new casting Oscar.
