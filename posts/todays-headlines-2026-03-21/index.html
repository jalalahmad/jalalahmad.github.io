    World News#

    U.S. weighs military reinforcements as the Iran war enters a new phase#

    Reuters reports the Trump administration is considering additional troop deployments and maritime operations around the Strait of Hormuz and Kharg Island as conflict risks widen. Officials said no final decision has been made, but the options signal concern about prolonged regional escalation.

    Rwanda and Congo agree to steps to de-escalate tensions after Washington talks#

    Reuters says representatives from Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo agreed to coordinated de-escalation steps, including force disengagement measures and civilian protections. The talks marked their first high-level encounter since new U.S. sanctions tied to the eastern Congo conflict.

    U.S. Politics#

    DHS shutdown standoff continues as airport disruptions intensify#

    NBC News and CNN report bipartisan negotiations over Homeland Security funding are still deadlocked, with sharp disagreement over ICE enforcement reforms. The prolonged impasse has left TSA staffing strained and pushed wait times higher at major airports.

    Tech & AI#

    Microsoft weighs legal action over Amazon-OpenAI cloud deal#

    Reuters reports Microsoft is considering legal options after Amazon and OpenAI signed agreements that could conflict with Microsoft’s cloud exclusivity terms for OpenAI services. The dispute underscores growing competition over enterprise and government AI infrastructure.

    Business#

    Fed holds interest rates steady and warns energy shocks could lift inflation#

    Reuters reports the Federal Reserve kept rates unchanged while projecting higher inflation and emphasizing uncertainty tied to the Iran conflict and oil volatility. Markets have pushed back expectations for rate cuts as energy costs stay elevated.

    Weather#

    Extreme U.S. weather brings heat records, blizzards, and storm risks#

    NPR, NBC News, and CNN report a nationwide weather pattern is driving triple-digit heat in parts of the West while storms and blizzard conditions disrupt travel elsewhere. Forecasters warned of ongoing severe-weather threats as rapid temperature swings continue.

    Culture#

    Oscars spread top honors as One Battle After Another and Sinners lead the night#

    NPR and CNN report One Battle After Another won Best Picture and Best Director, while Sinners took major awards including Best Actor for Michael B. Jordan. The ceremony also featured historic wins in categories like cinematography and animation-related music.
